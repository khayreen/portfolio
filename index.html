Web · FYP · 2026

Secure UiTM Credit Exemption Management System Using Optical Character Recognition and Multifactor Authentication

★ Best Report AwardFaculty of Computer Science and Mathematics (FSKM)

Automates diploma-to-degree credit transfer at UiTM. OCR extracts subjects, grades and credit hours from uploaded transcripts, parsing the file directly when the PDF is digital and passing it to Google Cloud Vision when it is scanned. Since subject titles are rarely identical across institutions, course equivalency is scored by a weighted three-algorithm service combining Levenshtein distance, Jaccard index and similar-text percentage. Seven user roles sit behind RBAC middleware, TOTP two-factor authentication and SHA-256 document signatures.
